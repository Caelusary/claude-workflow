**Principle:** When content does all the visual work the app itself has no identity. Pick a few physical objects from the domain and make them the UI's recurring forms, routing empty, error and not-found states through them. Presentations likewise take their visuals from the work's own figures, data and renders, in the register of the field, with each accent colour meaning one thing.
**Why it recurs:** Generic components and stock visuals fill the gaps wherever content is absent.
**Check:** Blank out the user content or text: is the app or deck still recognisable, and could it belong to any other project? Do empty, error and not-found states share one designed form?
