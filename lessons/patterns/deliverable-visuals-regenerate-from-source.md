title: "Every image of a living product comes from a re-runnable script"
status: open
kind: quality
dimension: theme-feel
seen: 1
---
**Principle:** When a deck or document shows a product still being built, every screenshot comes from a re-runnable capture script with fixed viewport and states, and product renders are captured on a transparent background so they sit on any slide.
**Why it recurs:** Manual screenshots go stale the moment the product changes and nobody remembers how they were taken.
**Check:** If the product changes tomorrow, can every image in this deliverable be regenerated with one command? Do renders show a background edge on dark and light backgrounds?
