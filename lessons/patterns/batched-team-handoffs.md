**Principle:** Keep a running change log, split it into small self-contained batches per teammate with exact steps and a verification command, and never commit under someone else's name.
**Why it recurs:** One large undifferentiated change cannot be reviewed or verified by any single person.
**Check:** Does each teammate have steps they can finish and verify alone?
