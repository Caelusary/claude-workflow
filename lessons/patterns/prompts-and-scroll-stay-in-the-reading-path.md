title: "Questions, answers and scrolling stay where the user is looking"
status: open

**Principle:** A prompt that needs a decision is not a toast: it sits in the reading path, moves content instead of covering it, can be dismissed without blocking, and whatever its answer unlocks takes over the same spot, with each action offered once. On screens with step-after-step decisions the question stays above what the answers build up, and long lists grow into the page with sticky context rather than scrolling inside fixed-height boxes.
**Why it recurs:** Designers place prompts by what is convenient to render, and nested scroll areas latch the wheel, so users must hunt and scroll to answer.
**Check:** For each question the app asks: is it somewhere people look, does it avoid covering UI, what happens if never answered, and is everything its answer unlocks visible in one place, offered once? Is there any fixed-height scroll area inside a scrolling page?
