**Principle:** Before writing anything that detects things in the physical world (camera, sensor, audio), obtain one real sample from the actual target and make the synthetic tests look like it.
**Why it recurs:** Assumptions about how the target looks are the first thing reality breaks, and synthetic tests built on them pass while the product fails.
**Check:** What is the one real photo, clip or reading of the actual target, and do the tests' fake inputs look like it?
