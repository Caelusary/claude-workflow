title: "Strong identity and motion, with a way down"
status: open
kind: quality
dimension: theme-feel
seen: 2
---
**Principle:** A committed identity, purposeful motion and depth make an app feel alive, and screen changes should move briefly rather than teleport. Every heavy effect needs a graceful way down so the feel never costs usability, and reduced-motion users get the same flow.
**Why it recurs:** Effects are added for delight and never tested without the delight.
**Check:** Would someone recognise this app from a screenshot? Does it still work well with effects off and reduced motion? Does any screen change snap with no motion?
